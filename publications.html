Study of UV Line and Continuum Variabilities in the Broadline Seyfert 1 Galaxy ESO 141–G55
Astronomische Nachrichten

Results from a three-year ultraviolet monitoring campaign of the broadline Seyfert 1 galaxy ESO 141–G55 using the International Ultraviolet Explorer (IUE). Individual spectra were modelled to trace continuum and emission-line variability, using JAVELIN for reverberation mapping and HEASOFT for spectral fitting with power-law and Lorentzian models.

doi.org/10.1002/asna.70093

STARSFLUX: an all-sky catalogue of absolute spectro-photometric calibrators from 0.3 to 30 μm
Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society

Obtaining accurate fluxes of faint sources from the ground in near- and mid-infrared wavelengths is challenging because of the rapidly changing atmospheric absorption. A common limitation is the lack of a nearby spectro-photometric calibrator. We present the STellar Absolute Reference Spectroscopic Flux Library (starsflux), an all-sky catalogue of calibrator spectra spanning 0.3–30 μm and comprising 64, 484 stars; the target list is based on the Mid-infrared stellar Diameters and Fluxes compilation Catalogue (MDFC).

doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag1478
