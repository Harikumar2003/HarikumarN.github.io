Summer school

IAAS-2026 at IIST: a memorable summer school in astronomy

From 13–22 July 2026, I attended the 14th IIST Astronomy and Astrophysics School at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology in Thiruvananthapuram. It was a wonderful opportunity to learn from leading researchers, work with modern astronomy tools, and experience how collaborative research in space science really feels on the ground.

The programme covered astronomy and astrophysics, observational techniques, the interstellar medium, star formation, high-energy astrophysics, stellar evolution, galaxies, cosmology, and helio physics. We also took part in hands-on sessions using VIREO, ALADIN, and DS9, along with night-sky observations using an 8-inch telescope. It was a deeply enriching experience, made even more special by the conversations and friendships built with fellow students passionate about astronomy.
