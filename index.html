About

I'm a final-year Integrated M.Sc. Physics student at NIT Rourkela, currently working as a Master's Thesis Intern at the National Centre for Radio Astrophysics (NCRA-TIFR). My work sits at the intersection of time-domain astronomy and AGN physics — using survey photometry, archival UV spectroscopy, and reverberation-mapping techniques to understand how light from the inner regions of quasars varies, and what that tells us about the accretion disk and black hole driving it. Outside of research, I lead astrophotography outreach with AstroNITR, stacking images of the Moon, Jupiter, and Saturn and helping hundreds of students look through a telescope for the first time.

The same photons that leave a quasar's accretion disk carry a record of its size, its geometry, and its history — if you watch them flicker for long enough. Working thesis — AGN variability, NCRA-TIFR, 2026
